# Bundled data

These files ship inside the APK as assets.

- `cities/<city>.json` — stations, lines and entrances. Generated by
  [`tools/fetch_stations.py`](../tools/fetch_stations.py) from OpenStreetMap.
  Do not hand-edit; fix the data in OpenStreetMap and regenerate.

Station data © [OpenStreetMap contributors](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright),
available under the [Open Database License (ODbL)](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/).

## Bengaluru coverage (2026-10-21)

83 stations: Purple 37, Green 32, Yellow 16 (Majestic and RV Road are
interchanges). The Pink and Blue lines are not in OpenStreetMap as Namma Metro
routes yet; regenerate once they are mapped and open.
